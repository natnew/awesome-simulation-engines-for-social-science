# Awesome Simulation Engines for Social Science

A curated research and engineering map for AI-driven social simulation, synthetic populations, agent-based modelling, policy experiments, uncertainty modelling, and ethical risk discovery.

Social systems are adaptive, uncertain, partially observable, and non-linear. Traditional analytical tools often struggle to capture weak signals, emergent behaviour, feedback loops, and interactions across scales.

This repository maps the ideas, methods, systems, datasets, frameworks, and evaluation practices needed to build simulation engines for social science: tools that allow researchers, policymakers, and AI systems designers to explore possible futures under different interventions.

The goal is not prediction theatre.

The goal is disciplined exploration.
